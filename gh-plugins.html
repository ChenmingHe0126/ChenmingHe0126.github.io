GH Plug-in

DeadFish 差异生长、正交分支与最短路径工具。Tools for differential growth, orthogonal branching and shortest paths.

DeadFish 将差异生长、正交分支和 Dijkstra 最短路径封装为 Grasshopper 组件：差异生长生成可调密度的空间填充纹理，正交分支生成直角树状结构，Dijkstra 在节点与连线构成的图中寻找最短路径。DeadFish brings three algorithms into Grasshopper: differential growth generates space-filling patterns with adjustable density; orthogonal branching creates rectangular, tree-like structures; and Dijkstra finds shortest paths through a graph of nodes and connections.

另提供基于 Geometry3Sharp 的网格减面与体素化工具，用于形态生成和几何处理。Additional tools built on Geometry3Sharp provide mesh reduction and voxelization for geometry processing.

Moyu 2D / 3D 拓扑优化，以及四款 Rhino 小游戏。2D / 3D topology optimization and four games inside Rhino.

提供 2D 像素与 3D 体素拓扑优化。定义设计域、荷载、支撑和保留区域后，用 SIMP 方法迭代调整材料密度，在材料用量约束下提高结构刚度；结果可输出为轮廓曲线、体素或等值面网格。Moyu offers 2D pixel and 3D voxel topology optimization. Define the domain, loads, supports and preserved regions, then use SIMP to iteratively redistribute material density for stiffness under a material-volume constraint. Results can be extracted as contour curves, voxels or an isosurface mesh.

另有叠楼、贪吃蛇、打砖块和雨燕四款 Rhino 视口游戏，均带老板键，可切换成体量、景观、立面或剖面研究画面。It also includes four Rhino viewport games: falling blocks, Snake, brick breaker and a flying game. Each has a boss key that switches to a massing, landscape, facade or section study.
