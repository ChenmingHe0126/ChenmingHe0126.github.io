DeadFish

从我硕士阶段的研究中发展而来的 Grasshopper 工具集，用于生成图案与图网络探索。主要包括差异生长、正交分支和 Dijkstra 最短路径，也提供网格减面与体素化工具。A Grasshopper toolkit for generative patterns and graph exploration, developed from my master's research. It brings together differential growth, orthogonal branching and Dijkstra shortest paths, with additional tools for mesh reduction and voxelization.

  • 差异生长Differential growth
  • 正交分支Orthogonal branching
  • 最短路径Shortest paths

Moyu

一个把实时 2D、3D 拓扑优化和四款 Rhino 视口小游戏放在一起的 Grasshopper 插件。可以用 SIMP 求解器探索材料分布，也可以玩叠楼、贪吃蛇、打砖块和雨燕；每款游戏都有老板键，一按切换成建筑研究画面。A Grasshopper plug-in combining live 2D and 3D topology optimization with four games inside the Rhino viewport. Explore material layouts with a SIMP solver, or take a break with falling blocks, Snake, brick breaker and a flying game—each with a boss key that switches to an architectural study.

  • 2D / 3D 拓扑优化2D / 3D topology optimization
  • Rhino 视口游戏Rhino viewport games
  • 老板键Boss key
